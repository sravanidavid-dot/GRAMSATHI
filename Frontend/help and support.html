Frequently Asked Questions

How can I raise a complaint?

+
Go to the Raise Complaint section from the dashboard. Enter the complaint details, provide the location and submit your complaint.

How can I track my complaint?

+
Open the My Complaints section to view your complaints and select a complaint to see its current status and tracking timeline.

What does Pending mean?

+
Pending means your complaint has been successfully submitted but action has not yet started.

What does In Progress mean?

+
In Progress means the concerned officer has received the complaint and action is currently being taken.

What does Resolved mean?

+
Resolved means the reported issue has been addressed or completed by the concerned authority.

Where can I see my notifications?

+
Open the Notifications section from the dashboard. You can see complaint submission, status updates and other notifications there.
